## Shared-module sheet

The v02 shared library, original solar asset, power-node kit, antenna kit and anti-missile pod were
used as references. The prompt requested seven exact labelled families: `TER-GLZ`, `TER-SOL`,
`TER-ROT`, `TER-WPN`, `TER-PWR`, `TER-LCH` and `TER-ANT`. It specified opaque framed glazing;
rigid solar panels and folded roots; S2/S3/S4 enclosed traverses; enclosed receivers and thick
barrels; inactive power equipment; closed 2x2/3x3/4x4 launch pods; and thick dishes, block radar
heads and flat sensor panels. The approved Terran palette, matte finish, closed silhouettes and
neutral presentation were locked.
