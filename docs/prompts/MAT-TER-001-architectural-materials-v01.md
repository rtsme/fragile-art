Mode: built-in image generation. All four outputs are project-bound seamless base-colour sources.

Shared constraints: flat orthographic surface, even neutral illumination, no directional light,
shadow, highlight, perspective, large unique mark, panel seam, border, bolt, rivet, functional
detail, text, logo, decal, baked lighting, ambient occlusion, gradient or vignette.

Perfectly seamless warm off-white Terran ceramic-coated structural armour with fine orange-peel
paint grain, extremely subtle micro-scratches, sparse soft grime variation and broad uniform
coverage.

## Graphite

Perfectly seamless dark graphite exposed structural steel, never pure black, with fine bead-blasted
grain, extremely subtle brushed variation and sparse soft wear.

## Concrete

Perfectly seamless restrained cool-grey reinforced concrete with fine aggregate, tiny pores,
subtle casting variation and clean military construction; no large cracks or stains.

## Amber

Perfectly seamless restrained amber safety paint on metal, muted industrial ochre-orange rather
than neon, with fine matte paint grain and very subtle micro-scratches.
