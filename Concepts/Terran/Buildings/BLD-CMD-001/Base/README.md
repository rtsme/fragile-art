# BLD-CMD-001 base-building source

`BLD-CMD-001_base-concept_v01.png` is the body-only generation source for the Command Centre
pipeline trial. It is derived from, but does not replace,
`../BLD-CMD-001_concept_v01.png`.

## Keep in the base

- foundation slab and primary footprint
- load-bearing walls and roofs
- central command-block mass and stepped roof hierarchy
- broad armour masses, deep setbacks and major chamfers
- large buttresses that are continuous with and define the wall silhouette
- shallow closed receiver recesses and simple S1-S4 mounting pads

## Keep out of the base

- glazing and window frames
- doors, airlocks, hatches and entrance portals
- dishes, radar heads, antenna masts and aerials
- tanks, pipes, valves, vents, fans and heat exchangers
- stairs, ramps, railings, ladders and catwalks
- lights, beacons, access panels, equipment boxes and surface greeble
- decals, markings, emissive effects and material wear

Generate future orthographic references from this simplified design, not from the fully dressed
final concept. The exact attachments are controlled by
`../BLD-CMD-001_assembly-manifest_v01.md`.
